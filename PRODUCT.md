# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain HTML/CSS/JS (static), deployed to GitHub Pages. Chosen by the user: single-folder site, no build step, no backend.

## Users

People in Brazil (content in pt-BR). Two audiences:

- Buyers looking for unique handmade ceramic pieces for their home or as gifts.
- People who want to learn pottery hands-on through classes and workshops.

## Product Purpose

Poca is a ceramics studio that makes and sells handmade pieces and teaches the craft. The website's job is to let a visitor see the work, start a conversation to buy a piece, and book a class or workshop — all through a message.

## Positioning

Every piece is made by hand in the studio, and the people teaching the classes are the same people making the work. A visitor can buy a piece or book a seat by messaging the studio directly.

## Operating Context

A ceramics studio: the wheel, kiln, clay, and glaze. Buying and booking are inquiry-based: a visitor sends a WhatsApp message or an Instagram DM (pre-filled with the piece, class, or date they are interested in), and the studio answers. There is no cart, checkout, or online payment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site, no server or backend; all commerce and booking is inquiry-based via WhatsApp and Instagram DM deep links.
- Multi-page structure: Início, Peças (galeria), Cursos & Aulas, Loja, Contato.
- Content language: Portuguese (Brazil).
- Imagery: curated placeholder photography to start, to be replaced with the studio's real photos.
- Not yet provided (must not be fabricated): real WhatsApp number, Instagram handle, class/workshop names, schedules and prices, product names and prices.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Poca".
- Existing logo: wordmark + symbol, drawn in a warm clay/terracotta palette (roughly #D8B4A2, #E5C3B3, #8E7367, #5B4B43) on a white ground. The logo palette is binding; the rest of the identity is to be created around it.
- Contact channels the site must route to: WhatsApp and Instagram DM.

## Product Principles

- The work leads: a visitor should see the actual pieces and the craft before anything else.
- One message starts everything: every buy and every booking is a single tap away from WhatsApp or Instagram DM.
- Honest and grounded: no invented prices, schedules, testimonials, or customers.
- Warmth without cliché: the clay palette is real, but the presentation must not fall into the generic ceramics-site look.
- Every interaction is a path back to the studio, not a dead end.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Responsive across mobile and desktop (a large share of visitors will be on phones, where WhatsApp/DM deep links live).
- Semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible navigation and controls, sufficient color contrast, `lang="pt-BR"`.
